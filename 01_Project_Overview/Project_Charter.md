# Project Charter

## 1. Project Title

**Hotel PMS Implementation & Client Onboarding End to End Case Study**

---

## 2. Project Type

**Simulated Hotel PMS Implementation Case Study**

This project demonstrates an end to end approach to implementing a Property Management System (PMS) for a hotel.

The case study is created for portfolio and learning purposes and does not represent a live implementation of any specific PMS product.

---

## 3. Client Profile

**Client:** Azure Grand Business Hotel  
**Location:** Pune, Maharashtra, India  
**Property Type:** 4-Star Business Hotel  
**Room Inventory:** 120 Rooms  
**Project Duration:** 16 Weeks

### Hotel Departments

- Front Office
- Reservations
- Housekeeping
- Food & Beverage
- Finance
- Sales
- Management
- IT

---

## 4. Business Context

Azure Grand Business Hotel currently manages several
operational activities using Excel spreadsheets, manual
registers and standalone systems.

The hotel wants to move toward a centralized Property
Management System (PMS) to improve operational visibility,
reduce manual work and standardize processes across
departments.

---

## 5. Business Problems

The current operating model creates several challenges:

### Reservations
- Manual reservation entry
- Difficulty maintaining a single source of truth
- Risk of duplicate guest records
- Manual confirmation processes

### Front Office
- Manual room allocation
- Limited visibility of real-time room status
- Manual check-in and checkout activities

### Housekeeping
- Room-status updates are not consistently synchronized
- Front Office may not have immediate visibility of room readiness

### Food & Beverage
- Restaurant charges may require manual posting to guest accounts
- Risk of duplicate or incorrect data entry

### Finance
- Manual billing and reconciliation
- Limited visibility of outstanding guest balances

### Management
- Operational reports require manual consolidation
- Limited real-time visibility of hotel performance

---

## 6. Project Objectives

The project aims to design an implementation approach for a
centralized PMS that will:

1. Standardize reservation management.
2. Improve room availability visibility.
3. Support front-office check-in and checkout workflows.
4. Improve coordination between Front Office and Housekeeping.
5. Centralize guest profiles and stay information.
6. Support guest folio and billing management.
7. Enable POS-to-PMS transaction integration.
8. Improve data quality through structured migration.
9. Establish a formal UAT process.
10. Prepare hotel users for system adoption.
11. Establish a controlled go-live and hypercare process.
12. Provide management with standardized operational reporting.

## 12. Success Criteria

The implementation will be considered successful when:

- Critical hotel workflows are mapped and validated.
- PMS requirements are clearly documented.
- Major process gaps are identified and addressed.
- Sample hotel data can be prepared for migration.
- Key PMS workflows pass UAT scenarios.
- POS-to-PMS integration requirements are documented.
- Hotel users receive role-specific training.
- Go-live activities are clearly defined.
- Post-go-live support and issue management are established.

---

## 15. Portfolio Disclaimer

This is a **simulated portfolio case study** created to
demonstrate knowledge of hotel operations and the
methodology used in SaaS/PMS implementation projects.

It is not a representation of a live implementation,
customer engagement, or implementation of a specific
vendor's PMS product.

The project is designed to demonstrate transferable
skills that can be applied to a professional hotel PMS
implementation environment.
